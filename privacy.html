Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. What data the app uses

When you connect your TikTok account, the app receives an access token and a refresh token from TikTok, and your TikTok account identifier. These are used only to publish videos to your own profile.

2. How the data is stored

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private code repository and are never published on this website. The website itself does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

3. How the data is used

Data is used solely to authenticate with TikTok and publish the creator's own videos. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Videos

Videos are kept by the creator in the creator's own cloud storage and are sent to TikTok only at publishing time.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is connected. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions), and you can ask for stored data to be deleted by contacting us.

6. Children

The app is not intended for people under the minimum age required to use TikTok.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision.
