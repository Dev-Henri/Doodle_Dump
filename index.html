Doodle Dump Poster

Schedule your own drawing videos on your own TikTok account.

Doodle Dump Poster is a small tool that lets a creator publish their own original drawing videos to their own TikTok account on a schedule. The creator connects their TikTok account once through TikTok Login. After that, the app uploads pre-selected videos with a caption and hashtags defined by the creator.

How it works

  1. The creator clicks "Login with TikTok" and approves access on TikTok's own authorization screen.
  2. The app receives an authorization code and exchanges it for an access token.
  3. The app publishes the creator's prepared videos to the creator's own profile using the Content Posting API.
